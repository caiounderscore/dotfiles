# Recurring patterns & drills (Caio)

Targeted material for the english-teacher skill. PT = Portuguese interference. Pull drills
from here; log every miss to `~/.agents/state/language/english-mistakes-log.md`.

## 1. Spelling of PT-cognates (top recurring)
Cognates that look almost like Portuguese but differ in English:
| ✅ English | ❌ seen | PT |
|---|---|---|
| recommend | recomend | recomendar |
| exercises | exercices | exercícios |
| mandatory | mandotory | mandatório |
| recurrent / recurring | recorrent | recorrente |
| errors | erros | erros |
| option | otpion | opção |
| focus | fucus | foco |
| AI | IA | IA |
- Rule of thumb: English doubles/spells differently — slow down on -mm-, -rr-, -ss-, and word endings.
- Drill: *I recommend the mandatory exercises to fix recurring errors.* (write it 1×)

## 2. Dropped "to be" / auxiliary
PT often omits the explicit verb; English requires it.
- ❌ *we talking* → ✅ *we're talking* · ❌ *the test it 2 hours* → ✅ *the test **is** in 2 hours*
- ❌ *the test identical* → ✅ *the test **is** identical*
- Drill: *We are in a meeting; the deploy is at 3 p.m.*

## 3. Articles (a / an / the)
PT drops/uses articles differently.
- ❌ *on documents folder* → ✅ *in **the** documents folder*
- ✅ *write **an** ADR* · *open **a** PR* · *check **the** logs*
- Drill: *Open a PR and put the diagram in the docs folder.*

## 4. Prepositions & collocations (fixed pairs)
| ✅ | ❌ |
|---|---|
| preferable **to** | preferable than |
| identical **to** | identical from |
| depend **on** | depend of |
| focus **on** | focus in |
| responsible **for** | responsible of |
| good **at** | good in |
| interested **in** | interested on |
- Drill: *This approach is preferable to the other; it depends on the load and focuses on cost.*

## 5. Subject–verb agreement
- ❌ *the topics … is* → ✅ *the topics **are*** · ❌ *the data are/is* (treat as your team's convention)
- Drill: *The metrics are noisy, but the root cause is clear.*

## 6. PT word order / literal translation
Rephrase instead of translating:
- ❌ *I have 10 years working with…* → ✅ *I have 10 years of experience working with…*
- ❌ *make a question* → ✅ *ask a question* · ❌ *take a decision* → ✅ *make a decision*

## Professional-English snippets (his real contexts)
- **Standup:** *Yesterday I … ; today I'll … ; I'm blocked on … .*
- **PR description:** *What & why → how to test → risk/rollback.* Use imperative commit subjects: *Add…, Fix…, Refactor… .*
- **Incident/postmortem:** *Impact, timeline, root cause, remediation, action items.* Past tense, blameless, factual.
- **ADR:** *Context → Decision → Alternatives considered → Consequences (positive/negative).*
- **Hedging/precision (native):** *roughly, broadly, in practice, by and large, as a rule of thumb, it depends on … .*
